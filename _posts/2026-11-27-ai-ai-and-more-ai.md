We all know what it is, what it does and why we should not use it.
Still we do. "Why?" you may ask.

**That's how I feel about it**:
First of all AI was or is one of the greatest inventions of humanity, you may disagree, but that's how I see it.

It's enabled us to do more, better and faster work than we ever could've thought.
Of course that does not mean that it is all good and there are no downsides.
Mainly when corporate managers think AI can replace their whole marketing team, your auntie posts the 500th AI slop post about astrology OR - the obvious - the whole ressource thing.

AI uses way too much electrical power and water (and money) to run. Whole powerplants have been built to serve the AI-data-center-gods. I don't have to explain that.
Still many of us (developers, writers, business-owners, the list goes on) use it almost daily to an extend that is not healthy for us nor the planet we all share.
It helps us debug, write quick emails we don't want to spend any more time on than we need, develop boilerplate code etc. Or in my case, code everything I ever wanted to have but I'm too lazy to actually sit through it because I can't keep the focus up. Thank you dopamine.

But don't worry, I at least write this post myself. Even though Claude has coded everything around it; my website, the tools I use to write this post which then gets automatically published to Github and in return my blog. -But I digress, as I do so often.

You may see where I'm going with this.
I do not per se like AI nor do I hate it. It is a helpful slave that builds the thousands of ideas that I come up with everyday, well at least 1% of them. And I actually stick with those better than if I would have to put my own time into them. (Dopamine)

We don't really see the consequences of our use of AI. Sure we know about it, everyone hates it, we tell everyone how bad it is for our environment.
Yet we don't see any of it, if we are living in a 1st world country like I do. At least not yet ...

However, I think we (and I count myself in too) should make ourselves more aware of the outcomes of our AI use.
Do we really need Claude (or any other AI) to code away for hours on end, only for us to have a project that we never touch again?

Not really.

That's one reason why I concentrate on a few projects at this point in time.
I am building (hah, I let AI build it) tools I actually use.

Mainframe, my project management TUI tool, which I'm writing this post in.

Terminaal, the terminal emulator I use everyday.

Watcher, the monitoring software I use in my homelab.

And then there is Klar.. the child I always wanted but I left to fail.
Not because I wanted it to fail, but because I don't really see any point of continueing. Continuing? Ah whatever.
An EU-based socialmedia platform in direct competition to Instagram and many other projects others have already developed and put out there.
There is soo much to it. I would've **never** thought. But I guess that's also part of all the learning.

As you can see, I mainly put all the trash on my mind in these posts. I digress, talk about all the other things instead of the main topic, well almost.

In summary AI is cool but also wasteful. We should not use it, still we do, because we don't really get to see the misery it's responsible for. Soil gets dryer every year, less water, colder winters, hotter summers bla bla bla.

We as humans will never learn from our mistakes, so might as well just continue using AI, dying of old age as one of the last generations, before we starve to death (remember, 1st world countries, everyone else.. AAH).

Weltschmerz is maybe the correct feeling when it comes to AI and the current development in human society.

Peace out I guess. See you in a bit.

Until then, stay afloat, do what makes you happy, help others, share, talk, play.
We don't know when it ends.
