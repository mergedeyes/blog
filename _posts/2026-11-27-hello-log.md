This is `~/log`, the part of [mergedcloud.de](https://mergedcloud.de/) where things get written down: homelab notes, things that broke, and how they got fixed.

It speaks a bit of [IndieWeb](https://indieweb.org/): posts are marked up with microformats, and if you write a reply on your own site, it can show up here via [webmention](https://indieweb.org/Webmention).

No promises about how often anything appears here.
